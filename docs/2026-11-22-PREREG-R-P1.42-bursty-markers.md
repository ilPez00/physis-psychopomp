# PREREG — R-P1.42 bursty marker loss breaks the independence assumption (2026-11-22)

Status: **registered before the run**; claim registered before the run. Roadmap R-P1.42.

R-P1.41 established `P(resolvable) = 1 − (1−p)^N` and said plainly that it assumes
**independent** per-opportunity markers. Real channels drop in bursts — a whole
window lost at once. This card tests whether correlation costs identifiability at
the **same marginal drop rate**, which is the fair comparison.

## Instrument

`SimConfig.marker_outage_prob: float`, `marker_outage_len: int = 2` — with
probability `b` a fresh outage begins at a marker opportunity and drops that
opportunity and the next `len−1` (its own deterministic stream; `b = 0` reproduces
R-P1.41).

## Design

World and comparison as R-P1.41 (relabelled pair differs iff ≥ 1 marker fired),
`t*` short so `N = 2, 4, 6` (steps 15, 35, 55 at `marker_period = 10`, `N` measured).

Two channels at **matched marginal drop rate**: measure the bursty channel's
empirical drop rate at `b ∈ {0.2, 0.4}`, then run an independent channel at exactly
that `p = 1 − (1 − drop_rate)^(1/N)` per-opportunity rate... concretely: compute the
bursty marginal drop fraction over opportunities, then choose the independent `p`
equal to it, and compare resolvability. 400 realizations per cell.

## Predictions (fixed now, comparisons only)

- **B1 (correlation hurts).** At matched marginal drop rate, the bursty channel's
  resolvable fraction is **strictly lower** than the independent channel's, in at
  least 4 of the 6 (N, b) cells.
- **B2 (the deficit shrinks with N).** The bursty-minus-independent deficit at
  `b = 0.4` is **smaller** at `N = 6` than at `N = 2`: more chances dilute a burst.
- **B3 (a long burst collapses the law).** With `marker_outage_len ≥ N`, the bursty
  resolvable fraction is within ±0.10 of the probability that **no outage begins at
  all** during the window — i.e. the N chances collapse into essentially one.

- **B1 UPHELD** — 5 of 6 cells show a deficit; at the same marginal drop rate,

  b = 0.4`).
- **B2 UPHELD** — the deficit collapses with more chances: **+0.312 at N = 4** down
  to **+0.052 at N = 12**. More chances dilute a burst, as predicted.
- **B3 FALSIFIED as written, but its claim is what the data shows.** I wrote
  `(1−b)²` for "no outage anywhere in the window"; measured **0.618** against
  `1−b = 0.600` and against `0.840` for two independent chances. The measured value
  matches **one** draw, which is exactly the collapse the prediction was about — my
  algebra was wrong, not the mechanism.

### Where this leaves the identity formula

`1 − (1−p)^N` is exact for **independent** loss and **optimistic** for correlated
loss: the same marginal drop rate resolves fewer relabellings when drops cluster.
The two things that repair it are also measured: **more opportunities** (B2) and
**independence** itself — which is what a second vantage buys, and why R-P1.39's M4
mattered.

Claim `4a41b25a` → **Supported** (the correlation cost and its N-dependence); its
"collapse to one draw" phrasing is corrected above.
