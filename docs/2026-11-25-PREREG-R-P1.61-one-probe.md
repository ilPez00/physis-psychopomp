# Preregistration — R-P1.61: one noisy diagnostic against passive repair

Claim `2fdd97c9-c1ee-4870-823f-84219d78f45e`. Prior **0.5**.
Freeze on origin before implementation or measurement. Status at freeze:
**not measured**. Follows R-P1.60, which returned `NO HEADROOM` because the
surviving-world counts carry no signal this fixture can reach.

## Why one probe, and why not a ranker

R-P1.60 measured that ranking candidates by surviving-world support is at the
null on development and worse than silence under completion error. More of the
same counts cannot help: under the generator's uniform prior over valid graphs
and a uniform three-edge mask, every surviving world is equally likely, so the
support count **is** the conditional edge marginal and the top-3 rule already
maximises expected hits at that budget. No learner trained on those counts can
beat it. The missing ingredient is information, not fitting.

This card buys exactly one unit of information and asks what it is worth.

## Fixture

Identical to R-P1.60 (`scripts/repair-eval-prereg.py`): 24 seeds, split
`0–11` development and `12–23` held-out, 8 entities, a true edge set of 6
`LINK`s over kind-admissible pairs, closure rule no in/out degree above 2,
observation of every entity plus 3 of the 6 true edges, candidate pool from
`psychopomp.graph_patch.decode`. Mean pool 21.5 edges, 3 missing true edges per
case, 0 determined. Labels live in the scorer and the simulated instrument only.

## Instrument (declared, not measured)

One query per case on one candidate edge `e ∈ P`. The reading is `present` or
`absent`:

```
P(present | e ∈ truth) = 0.8      P(present | e ∉ truth) = 0.2
```

Independent per query, seeded `Random(seed*104729 + 7)`. This is a declared
sensor law used to simulate the world; nothing about a real instrument is
claimed, and the card's numbers are conditional on it.

Posterior over surviving worlds: uniform before the reading, uniform over the
worlds consistent with it after (the closure rule is already in the candidate
set). An edge's score is its posterior mass. Digits: float64, ties broken by
`(source, predicate, target)` ascending.

## Arms (same cases, same candidate sets, one query where stated)

- **A — no repair:** propose nothing. Zero queries.
- **B — passive support:** top 3 by surviving-world support. Zero queries.
- **B1 — cost-matched passive:** one query on a uniformly random candidate; the
  reading is discarded; then B's rule. One query, so B's free information cannot
  be confused with D's paid information.
- **C — random probe:** one query on a uniformly random candidate; update; top 3
  by posterior mass. One query.
- **D — active probe:** query `argmax` expected information gain over the
  uniform world posterior,
  `H(W) − E_r[H(W | r)]`, ties by edge key; update; top 3 by posterior mass.
  One query.
- **E — permuted-edge control:** D's query and D's reading, applied to a
  uniformly chosen different candidate in `P \ {e}`. The marginal distribution
  of readings is exactly D's; only the readout-to-edge association is destroyed.
  One query.
- **F — uninformative-probe control:** D's query, reading drawn `0.5/0.5`
  regardless of truth; update; top 3. One query. 200 draws, mean reported.

## Metrics (every figure prints its denominator)

Per arm and split: proposed, correct recoveries, false corrections, precision
(`— (0 proposals)` when nothing is proposed, never `0.000`), recall over the 3
missing true edges per case, abstentions, **completion error** over the
candidate pool, and queries used. Plus, for C/D/E: **predicted vs measured** hit
rate of the top-3 posterior mass, and the mean posterior mass assigned to the
three true missing edges.

## Frozen checks

- **H1 — the fixture is ambiguous.** 0 determined cases, ≥ 2 surviving worlds
  each, identical to R-P1.60's H1. Failure ⇒ **VACUOUS**.
- **H2 — no leak through the probe choice.** Held-out F completion error is not
  better than B's by more than **0.01**. Failure ⇒ **INVALID**: an
  uninformative reading cannot produce a gain, so something other than the
  reading is doing the work.
- **H3 — one correctly-paired probe is worth having.** Held-out D completion
  error ≤ B's − **0.05**, and D precision ≥ B precision + **0.10**.
- **H4 — the association is what pays.** Held-out D completion error ≤ E's −
  **0.05**. E matches D's reading marginal, so a gain must come from knowing
  *which edge* the reading is about.
- **H5 — choosing the edge matters** (reported, not a gate for H3/H4): D versus
  C. If D − C < 0.03, record "selection adds nothing at this sensor quality".
- **H6 — the posterior is calibrated** (reported): |predicted − measured| top-3
  hit rate ≤ **0.10** on held-out. A larger gap is recorded as miscalibration
  and is the finding; it is not repaired here.
- **Headroom:** HEADROOM only if H1, H2, H3 and H4 hold **and**
  `1 − D_precision ≥ 0.10`. Otherwise **NO HEADROOM**.

## Stop rules

- No threshold is retuned after measurement; a defective check is corrected in a
  new card only.
- H2 failure stops the card at INVALID with no arm verdict.
- No learner is trained in this card or as a result of it. If HEADROOM, E6 opens
  with D as the frozen baseline and must beat it at equal query cost.
- If NO HEADROOM, the next question is whether the sensor law or the single-query
  budget is the binding constraint, and that is a new card with its own claim —
  not a re-run with a better probe.

## Limits

- Synthetic graphs, one predicate, one closure rule, one observation budget, one
  declared sensor law, one query. Nothing transfers to a real repository graph
  or a real instrument without its own card.
- The probe reads a candidate **edge**, not the world; a real sensor's
  correlation structure, cost heterogeneity and failure modes are not modelled.
- A repair proposal is not an authorized write: the writer's evidence and
  approval boundary is separate and a learner never holds it.
