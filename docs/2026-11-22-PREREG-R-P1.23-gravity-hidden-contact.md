## The design fix, and why it is not retuning

R-P1.20's failure was geometric, not statistical: the blind region simply did not
reach the contact point. The honest fix is to place the world so it does:
extend the first occluder downward (`o = (40, 60, 20, 65)`), which lets the blind
region cover `y ≥ 20`, and keep the second occluder narrow (`o2 = (46, 54, 20, 65)`)
so it still *relocates* the blind spot. The contact then lands at
`y ≈ 33.8 ∈ [20, 65]` and `x = 50 ∈ [46, 54]` — hidden.

This is a fresh prereg on a fresh geometry, not an amendment to R-P1.20: no
threshold, hypothesis or cell of that card changes, and its falsification stands.

*world plus observer set* it is X, and change the observer set and it is not".
