## Instrument

One acquisition maximum per episode; no retry. Two equiprobable laws −1/+1
and four equally weighted sensor modes per law. Ordered readings by mode:

| Probe | Cost | Law −1 | Law +1 |
|---|---:|---|---|
| passive | 1 | 0, 0, 0, 0 | 0, 0, 0, 0 |
| overlap | 1 | −2, −2, −1, +1 | +2, +2, −1, +1 |
| clean | 2 | −2, −2, −2, −2 | +2, +2, +2, +2 |

Choose maximum strictly positive predicted law information gain among
affordable probes; keep the prior on missing or unsupported readings.
Declare a law only at posterior probability one. Only the fixture receives
the law and mode. For fixed control, select passive. For random control,
enumerate both affordable probes at budget 1, equally weighted. For the
shuffled control, execute the overlap probe but replace the prediction
labels by a balanced reassignment: each law gets half the pooled count of
each reading. Thus it sees the same physical readings, cost and unconditional
histogram, but the prediction table carries no law information. This measures
law information, not utility or full expected free energy.

scoring other checks. Use exact counts and rational probabilities; compare
entropy values within `1e-12`. Preserve failures without adjusting the
catalogue. Exhaustive discrete witness only: not empirical noise calibration,
