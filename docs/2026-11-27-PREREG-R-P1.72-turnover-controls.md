## The fixture, unchanged

`psychopomp/turnover.py` as R-P1.49 froze it: `run(arm, target, perturbation, *,
relabel, nominal)`, arms `feedback` / `open-loop`, cells `(target ∈ {1,2}, p ∈
{0,1,2})`, `OBSERVE` ticks, `residence` as the metric, `balanced` as the
residence predicate. R-P1.49's measured result is the anchor: at `p = 2`,
`feedback` residence 3 and `open-loop` residence 0, on 3/3 observe ticks.

## The three controls, and what each must show

1. **Feedback removal.** Run the `feedback` arm with its corrective transfer
   suppressed from tick 1 — i.e. the organisation cannot act on imbalance. It must
   then behave like `open-loop`: residence 0 at `p = 2`. If a "feedback" arm that
   cannot feed back still recovers, the measured difference was never feedback.
2. **Sham.** Perturb with the *sign flipped* (`p = -2`): both roles displaced, no
   net imbalance. Residence must be **3** in both arms — the discriminating cell
   needs an imbalance, not merely a disturbance. This is the control that says the
   effect is about balance rather than about change.
3. **Independent copy.** Run two `feedback` arms from **independent** initial
   inventories (different component counts) and require that their recoveries are
   *not* equal — i.e. the result depends on the organisation's own state, not on a
   shared constant. Where the two happen to coincide, that must be reported as a
   tie rather than counted as agreement.

## Frozen predictions

- **P1 — the anchor holds.** The `p = 2` cell reproduces R-P1.49 exactly:
  `feedback` residence 3, `open-loop` 0, on 3/3 observe ticks. Anything else means
  the fixture moved and the run is `UNVERIFIABLE`.
- **P2 — feedback removal collapses to open-loop.** With corrective transfer
  suppressed, `feedback` residence at `p = 2` is **0**, matching `open-loop`.
- **P3 — the sham does not discriminate.** At `p = -2` both arms give residence
  **3**. The effect needs imbalance.
- **P4 — an independent copy is not the same organisation.** Two arms from
  different initial inventories produce **different** residence trajectories in at
  least one of the two targets. If they always coincide, R-P1.49 measured a
  constant, not an organisation.
- **P5 — no control writes state.** All three run without appending to a log, and
  the module's public functions remain pure over their declared inputs.

Any prediction may fail and is kept. **P3 failing would be the interesting one**:
a sham that discriminates would mean the metric responds to disturbance rather
than to imbalance, and R-P1.49's reading would need re-scoping.
