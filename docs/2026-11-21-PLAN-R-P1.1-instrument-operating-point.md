# Plan — R-P1.1 instrument operating point (2026-11-21, before execution)

Status: **PLAN**, written before the measurement. Predecessor: round 9
(`docs/2026-11-21-PLAN-round9.md`), roadmap `docs/ROADMAP.md` R-P1.1.

## Why

Round 9 established, with a measurement, that the A3 experiment is blocked by
the ratio of sensor noise to the separation of the two trajectories, not by
sample count: at r = sep/noise ≤ 0.4 the exact sensor likelihood cannot decide
any world, and `track(3)` samples turn the feasibility set empty because
velocity noise is amplified by 1/dt.

Mission P1's core slice (R-P1.2, multiple observation perspectives of the same
episode) needs an operating point where the observables **do** determine the
answer; otherwise every perspective arm inherits an abstention and measures
nothing. This plan fixes that operating point, with predictions written first.

## Predictions (fixed now; any of these failing is a result, not a bug to tune)

- **P1** — exact-Bayes decided-rate increases monotonically in r = sep/noise on
  the parallel-lane family; at r ≥ 5 every attributable episode is decided and
  top-1 = 1.00 (no contacts, 4 candidate worlds per recrossing pair).
- **P2** — at r ≤ 0.5 the decided count is 0 (consistent with round 9's
  abstention at r ≈ 0.4).
- **P3** — the A2 position-continuity probe scores 1.00 across the whole
  sweep: it consumes boundary positions, not derived velocities, so it is not
  affected by the 1/dt amplification.
- **P4** — top-1 accuracy is 1.00 wherever the likelihood decides (the
  decision and the answer are not independent questions at these cells).

If P1 is non-monotone, or P2 decides anything, the round-9 constraint story is
wrong; that gets written down before any cell is chosen.

## Instrument

- Corpus: parallel lanes, `Init(x=(30, 30+2*(k%5)), y=(45, 45+sep), v=((3,0),(3,0)))`,
  `SimConfig(steps=600, noise=noise)`, 6 attributable episodes per cell
  (worlds enumerated, truth pairing available).
- Tolerance: `max(noise, 1e-4)` — the filter's job is to enumerate; noise is
  the sensor's.
- Arms: exact Bayes (uniform, the true sensor), MAP (Gaussian surrogate),
  A2 position continuity, and the best unfitted statistic from
  `statistic_survey` (the "trivial statistic" round 7 warned about).
- Two axes: `noise = 2` with `sep ∈ {2,4,6,10,18}` (r = 1…9); `sep = 6` with
  `noise ∈ {0.5,1,2,3,6}` (r = 12…1). Ten cells, six episodes each.

## Gate

`scripts/instrument-sweep.py` prints the table; the operating point is the
**smallest sep and largest noise with decided = 6/6 and top-1 = 1.00** (cheap
sensor, small geometry). Not a registered claim — an instrument spec. Any
positive claim about a learned model still has to beat the deterministic
1.00 of A2 separately (mission P1 §4).

- **P1 upheld**: decided count is monotone non-decreasing in r
  (0, 0, 0, 2, 4, 6, 6, 6, 6 as r rises 0.25 → 12).
- **P2 upheld**: at r ≤ 0.33 the exact sensor likelihood decides nothing
  (tie 6/6), consistent with round 9.
- **P3 FALSIFIED**: the A2 position-continuity probe is **not** independent of
  the noise/separation ratio on this family — 0.33 at r ≤ 2, 0.67 at r = 3,
  0.83 at r = 5–6, and 1.00 only at r ≥ 9. The A2 reading is therefore a
  **high-r (full-precision geometry) result**, not a property of the position
  channel. A4 cannot borrow A2's 1.00 at a low r; it must fix the operating
  point first. This correction is the main output of the sweep.
- **P4 upheld**: top-1 is 1.00 wherever the likelihood decides (decision and
  correctness are one question at these cells).
- The "best statistic" column reaching 1.00 where exact Bayes abstains is
  **selection bias** (max over five statistics chosen per cell, six episodes);
  at r ≤ 0.33 it drops to 0.50. Read it as the round-7 warning, never as a
  decision rule.

### Operating point chosen (gate applied)

- EC/Bayes arms: **sep = 6, noise = 1 (r = 6)** — decided 6/6, top-1 1.00,
  MAP 1.00, A2 0.83.
- Arms that rely on the A2 position channel: **sep = 6, noise = 0.5 (r = 12)**
  — A2 1.00, Bayes 6/6.
- Rejected: every cell with r ≤ 3 (no or partial decisions). Nothing above
  r = 12 was needed; larger separation buys nothing extra.

R-P1.2 (perspectives) inherits this: noise 1.0 for Bayes/EC arms, 0.5 for the
position channel, sep 6. Not a registered claim — an instrument spec.
