# PREREG — R-P1.35 self-knowledge under noise (2026-11-22)

Status: **registered before the run**; claim registered in the store before the
run. Roadmap `docs/ROADMAP.md` R-P1.35.

Every self-model result so far is noise-free, where the one-step tolerance is the
only error. The law's own envelope starts at σ = 0.1 and runs to 40; the estimator
has never been asked what happens when the crossings it reads are corrupted. The
containment ("always optimistic") property is a *bound over exact evidence* — one
noisy outlier can defeat a `min`.

## Design

World: R-P1.29's single cell — `o2 = (46,54,35,65)`, blind `(46,54,35,65)`,
step 0.3. Corpus: horizontal and vertical sweeps as R-P1.29, but with sensor noise
`σ ∈ {0, 0.1, 0.3, 1.0}` on the observable log, **20 realizations** per σ
(episode index varied so the noise draws differ).

Three estimators on the same noisy logs:

1. **shipped** — `min`/`max` over crossing bounds (what R-P1.29–R-P1.34 used);
2. **robust** — 10 % / 90 % quantiles of the same bounds, so a single outlier
   cannot set the edge;
3. **abstaining** — the robust estimate, but reported only when the bound's spread
   is at most `2·step`; otherwise the edge is `None`.

Error is measured against the true edge, per edge, and signed: **pessimistic** =
the estimate covers more than the truth (`x0_hat < x0` or `x1_hat > x1`).

## Predictions (fixed now)

- **P1 (noise degrades the estimate).** Mean absolute edge error grows
  monotonically in σ for the shipped estimator.
- **P2 (containment breaks).** At σ = 1.0 (noise ≈ 3× the step) at least one
  **pessimistic** edge appears in the shipped estimator, in at least one
  realization. At σ ≤ 0.1 it stays contained in every realization.
  *This is the first predicted counterexample to the one-signed optimism: it holds
  for exact evidence, not for noisy evidence.*
- **P3 (robustness beats min/max).** At σ = 1.0 the robust estimator's pessimistic
  rate is **strictly lower** than the shipped one's.
- **P4 (abstention preserves the guarantee).** With the abstaining estimator, every
  edge it *does* report is contained in the truth in **≥ 95 %** of realizations at
  every σ, at the price of reporting fewer edges as σ grows.

## Prior

**0.8** for P1, **0.75** for P2 (a single corrupted crossing sets a `min`, so the
containment bound should break well before σ = 1.0), **0.7** for P3, **0.6** for
P4 — the 95 % bar with 20 realizations is tight, and the spread threshold `2·step`
is a guess that a noisy corpus might exceed at σ = 0.3 already, making the
abstaining estimator silent where it could have been useful.

## Rules

- No retune. A falsified prediction is a receipt.
- σ grid, 20 realizations and the 10 % quantile are declared here; nothing widened
  afterwards.

## Execution status — run 1, 2026-11-22: P2/P3 UPHELD, P1/P4 FALSIFIED

| estimator | σ=0 | σ=0.1 | σ=0.3 | σ=1.0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| shipped, mean abs edge error | 0.200 | 0.193 | 0.182 | 0.220 |
| shipped, pessimistic realizations | 0/20 | 0/20 | **4/20** | **14/20** |
| robust (q=0.1), pessimistic | 0/20 | 0/20 | 4/20 | **11/20** |
| abstaining, reported / contained | 20/20 | 20/20 | 20/20 (16) | **9/20 (5)** |

- **P1 FALSIFIED** — the mean error is **not monotone** in σ (0.200 → 0.193 →
  0.182 → 0.220). Up to σ = 1.0 the estimate is dominated by the systematic
  one-step quantisation (≈ 0.2), and noise barely moves the average; only the
  **tail** moves. Reading a mean would have hidden the effect entirely.
- **P2 UPHELD** — containment holds at σ ≤ 0.1 and **breaks at σ = 0.3**
  (4/20 realizations) and badly at σ = 1.0 (**14/20**). This is the first
  measured **pessimistic** self-model error: the one-signed optimism is a
  property of exact evidence.
- **P3 UPHELD** — a 10 % quantile bound reduces pessimism at σ = 1.0 from 14/20 to
  **11/20**. Real, but not a fix.
- **P4 FALSIFIED** — the spread-based abstention does not restore containment:
  it withholds reports (20 → 9 at σ = 1.0) but of the 9 it still reports, **4 are
  pessimistic** (5/9 contained = 56 %, far below the 95 % bar).

### The finding

> The containment guarantee is a property of **exact evidence**. Under noise the
> estimator becomes pessimistic (14/20 at σ = 1.0, first at σ = 0.3); a quantile
> bound softens it (14 → 11) and a spread threshold only reduces the number of
> reports without making the remaining ones honest.

**Knowing when you do not know is not enough when the evidence about your own
uncertainty is itself corrupted.** A calibrated self-model under noise needs a
noise model — an estimate of σ and a bound propagated through it — not a
heuristic threshold on the spread.

### The consequence for learning (and it is new)

For the first time in this program a **learned arm is licensed by measurement**
rather than by availability: at σ = 1.0 the deterministic estimator is pessimistic
in 70 % of realizations, so it is **not at ceiling**. Mission P1 §4 forbade a
learned model where a deterministic arm already scored 1.00; here it does not, and
the operator reports the GPU node reachable (ports 22/11434 open from here; Ollama
reachable by IP even though non-interactive `ssh` hangs at the handshake). A
learned edge regressor over the crossing bounds — with a shuffled-control arm and
a deterministic baseline — is the honest next card, and it is justified by this
table, not by the machine being idle.
