## World, sensors and budgets

`Y = S + M·a`, with law `M ∈ {−1,+1}` and reset state `S ∈ {−3,−1,1,3}`.
Use independent uniform priors (each joint row has mass `1/8`). For
calibration only, the nominal no-response law is `M=0` and is outside the
rival prior. The sensor exposes only the declared scalar, action ID and phase;
never truth IDs, joint-row index, `S`, or other same-reset readouts.

Calibration costs one separate observation: `|Y|` at `a=4`. Nominal support is
`{1,3}`; rival support is `{1,3,5,7}`. Only readings `5` and `7` contradict the
nominal law. **Do not** treat `1` or `3` as a detected mismatch. Use all eight
rows to score calibration and initiate diagnosis only from rejected nominal
readings. All four rejecting rows, with two per rival, must leave `P(M=−1)=
P(M=+1)=1/2`. A reading outside all supports is `out_of_support`, not discovery.

Diagnosis costs at most one action and happens on a **fresh independent reset**
under the same law. Allowed equal-cost arms: `passive` (`a=0`, `Y`), `signed`
(`a=4`, `sign(Y)`), `magnitude` (`a=4`, `|Y|`). Missing diagnostic data
are `missing`, not a likelihood of zero. Diagnose each rival/reset combination
once and score against a sealed truth label only after the decision.

## Predictions and stop rules

- **E1, capacity:** the instrument agrees with the eight-row table. Calibration
  rejects the nominal model in exactly `4/8` rows and in `0/4` nominal cases;
  all eight rival magnitudes leave the rival law posterior `1/2 : 1/2`.
  Use distinct reasons for nominal-compatible and outside-all-support readings.
- **E2, epistemic target:** `I(M; readout), I(S; readout)` in bits are `(0,2)`
  for passive, `(1,0)` for signed, `(0,1)` for magnitude. Model-targeted
  selection picks signed; state-targeted selection picks passive. Maximum
  readout entropy ties passive and magnitude (uniform tie-break, zero law
  information). Uniform random over three arms averages `1/3` law bit exactly
  via enumeration, not a sampled random seed. Scores and ties must be
  invariant to candidate and arm order.
- **E3, verified resolution:** signed-only update resolves correctly on `8/8`
  diagnostic rows with no false claims. Passive and magnitude resolve `0/8`
  each. When signed is unavailable, or under the declared shuffle, there is
  no positive-gain law probe and the selector abstains (`0/8` discoveries).
  In the forced shuffled-signed control, the update also leaves the law prior
  unchanged on `8/8`. A missing reading or one outside the chosen arm's entire
  support yields no update and no discovery. Do not reinterpret a valid
  opposite sign as outside support; the sealed scorer would count a wrong result.

Evaluate posterior probabilities by exact rational counts; compare computed
entropy/gain within `1e-12` bits. Select a law probe only for gain greater than
`1e-12`. If E1 fails, stop scoring E2/E3 and record the failed capacity case.
Otherwise record all results as written; no retune or replacement of failed
cells. This is selection within two declared laws, not general law recovery
or full expected-free-energy planning.
