# Preregistration — R-P1.73: finite-radius crossing labels, and what a sliver costs

Claim `969e3ade-530c-4d53-9851-9cbe0e0a0c3c`, registered at **prior 0.5** before the run. Freeze on origin before
any measurement. Status at freeze: **not measured**.

D5's last unmeasured clause: *"finite-radius crossing labels and calibration."*
The mask half of D5 is answered — R-P1.70 found `BlindEstimate` is a rectangle by
construction, and R-P1.71 showed an event in a hole is identified like open space.
What has never been measured is what `visual_radius` does to the **labels** a
crossing emits, and whether the estimator's edge moves by exactly the radius.

## What the code does, verified before the card

`observable` reports a body hidden only when its **whole disc** is inside the
mask, and `_rect_bounds` reads the `pos` of `enter_occ` / `exit_occ` events. So
with `visual_radius = r`:

```
r=0.0  x=41 hidden   x=42 hidden      (point model: mask edge is 40)
r=1.0  x=41 hidden   x=42 hidden
r=2.0  x=41 visible  x=42 hidden      (eroded by 2: effective edge is 42)
```

measured on `o = (40, 60, 35, 65)` at `y = 50`. So the *effective* mask is eroded
by `r` on every side, and a crossing is reported where the **disc** fully enters,
not where the centre does. Two consequences are testable:

1. the inferred blind rectangle should shrink by exactly `r` per side;
2. a body that clips a corner emits **no** crossing at all, because its disc never
   fits inside — a sliver costs a label, not a small error.

## Design

R-P1.43's crossing corpus (two horizontal lanes, two vertical columns, the shape
that gives the estimator all four edges), at `r ∈ {0.0, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0}`, 5 seeds.

- **A — `r = 0`**: the point model. The anchor; its estimate is
  `(39.9, 60.1, 34.9, 65.0)` from R-P1.70.
- **B — `r > 0`**: same body paths, same mask, disc rule on.
- **C — corner clip**: a path that grazes a corner so that the disc is inside for
  no step at all, at `r = 0` and `r = 2`.

## Frozen predictions

- **P1 — the anchor reproduces.** At `r = 0` the estimate is the point-model
  rectangle (`39.9, 60.1, 34.9, 65.0` ± 0.05), matching R-P1.70.
- **P2 — the estimate shrinks by exactly the radius per side.** For each `r`, the
  four edges move inward by `r` within ±0.35: near edges increase, far edges
  decrease. Measured as four signed deltas, not as a verdict.
- **P3 — calibration is monotone, not affine.** The shrink tracks `r` across all
  three values (non-decreasing in `r` per edge), so a single `r` measurement
  cannot be extrapolated to another — the calibration clause the card's title
  names.
- **P4 — a sliver costs a label.** In the corner-clip arm at `r = 2`, the body
  emits **fewer** crossings than at `r = 0`, because its disc never fits inside.
  If the label count is unchanged, the disc rule is not reaching the emitter.
- **P5 — no oracle movement.** The oracle log is identical across `r`, since the
  radius is a sensor property, not a world one.

Any prediction may fail and is kept. **P2 failing would contradict the radius
documentation** and would be the more important finding.

## What this card may and may not conclude

- It **may** say whether the rectangle estimate calibrates to `visual_radius`, and
  quantify the sliver cost.
- It **may not** claim a radius-aware estimator: `BlindEstimate` has no radius
  field (R-P1.70), so this measures the bias a rectangle estimator carries when
  the sensor has extent.
- It **may not** generalise to non-rectangular masks — R-P1.71 covered one hole
  for the verdict only.
- `E6` stays closed.

## Controls and honesty

- `r = 0` anchors to a measured number from a different card, so "the fixture
  moved" is detectable.
- Each `r` changes one parameter and nothing else.
- The receipt records the estimate, the crossing count and the body paths, so P4
  is checkable as a count rather than asserted.
- Verdict vocabulary: `PASS` / `FAIL` / `UNVERIFIABLE`.

## Limits

One rectangle, one corpus shape, four radii. A mask with a hole plus a radius is
two seams at once and is not measured here.
