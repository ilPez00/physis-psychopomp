# PREREG — R-P1.12 per-event coverage, boundary-free grid (2026-11-22)

Status: **registered before the run**. Roadmap `docs/ROADMAP.md` R-P1.12.
R-P1.11 measured the per-event rule at **29/30**. The one miss is diagnosed, not
hand-waved: the cell `ov-c, x0=20, lane y=51` puts the event exactly on the
blind boundary, and the simulator's accumulated position at step 60 is
`41.99999999999992` while the analytic prediction used exact `42.0` — 1e-14
apart, on opposite sides of the predicate. The rule was not falsified; the
prediction arithmetic was. This card tests the rule again on a grid where that
ambiguity cannot arise.

## The rule (unchanged)

> An intervention is identifiable from the observable log **iff** its event
> location is not inside the blind region `o ∩ o2`.

**Boundary rule, declared before the run:** a cell whose event position lies
within `1e-6` of any blind-region boundary is *excluded from the rule* and
reported separately. No cell in this grid is designed to be near one; if one
turns out to be, it is excluded and named, not counted either way.

### The claim

> An intervention is identifiable from the observable log **iff** its event
> location is not inside the blind region `o ∩ o2`. Aggregate blind area is a
> proxy and carries no independent information.

Registered hypothesis `9624cc4a-5167-42b3-9f1a-39f131542a9d`: 29/30 out of
sample (R-P1.11, single miss a 1e-14 boundary artifact) then 30/30 on a
boundary-free grid (R-P1.12), with a control at chance in all 60 cells.

**Scope, stated as a limit:** this is one family — constant-velocity bodies,
rectangular occluders, one intervention, exact (noise-free) observations,
finite declared hypotheses. It says nothing about degraded observations, moving
observers, non-convex occluders or learned inference. What it does say is exact
and measured: **identifiability of an intervention is a per-event property of
the observer set.**
