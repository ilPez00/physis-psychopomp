# PREREG — R-P1.17 time-resolved coverage with a moving observer (2026-11-22)

Status: **registered before the run**. Roadmap `docs/ROADMAP.md` R-P1.17.
R-P1.12/R-P1.16 established the law for a **fixed** observer set: identifiable
iff the event location is outside the blind region `o ∩ o2`. This card asks the
temporal question: when the second observer *moves*, is the relevant coverage the
one **at the event step**, or does the whole history matter?

## Instrument change

`SimConfig.o2_velocity` — the second occluder translates rigidly at constant
velocity, folded inside the box (`_fold`, a triangle wave), so it reflects at the
walls deterministically. `occluder2_at(cfg, t)` gives the rect at step `t`, and
visibility at step `t` uses it. Default velocity `(0, 0)` reproduces the fixed
observer exactly.

**Counterfactuals hold the observer fixed**: candidates share the config, so the
occluder schedule is identical across hypotheses. If it were not, an observer
change would be confounded with a world change and the card would measure
nothing.

## Predictions (fixed now)

- **P1 (time-resolved rule).** All **11** noise-free cells match the analytic
  prediction exactly. *One mismatch falsifies.*
- **P2 (the flip is real).** `x = 44` is a tie when static and unique when the
  observer drifts right; `x = 55` is a tie when static and unique when it drifts
  left. Each flip is a *time* effect, not a geometry change: the same occluder
  and the same world, only different coverage at the event step.
- **P3 (reflection is tracked).** The `folded` cell reflects the occluder
  (`x` position before folding exceeds the box bound) and still obeys the rule.
- **P4 (noise does not change the pattern).** At σ = 1.0 with 100 realizations:
  uncovered cells ≥ 0.90 rank accuracy, covered cells within 0.5 ± 0.15.
- **P5 (observer held fixed).** For every hypothesis the occluder schedule is
  bit-identical to the observation's; asserted, not assumed.

## Prior

adds one dimension without new physics; the risk is a bookkeeping error in which
step the visibility is sampled at (the round-9 lesson: off-by-one in `t` is the
classic failure).

### One instrument bug found by the run

With a moving occluder a visibility change can occur with **no edge crossing**:
the rect sweeps onto or off a body. The old side-label lookup assumed a crossing
existed and raised `StopIteration` (caught on `left, E55`). Fixed with
`transition_rect()` returning `None` for a motion-caused change, labelled
**`sweep`** — a new, honest side value rather than a silently wrong `top`.

### The claim, extended

> Identifiability is **event-local coverage in space and time**: the intervention
> is identifiable iff its location at the event step is outside `o ∩ o2(t)`, with
> the observer held fixed across counterfactuals. A moving observer changes
> identifiability by moving coverage, not by being an observer.

Not measured: observers that *choose* their motion in response to the world
(active perception), parallax/perspective, non-rectangular occluders, more than
two observers.
