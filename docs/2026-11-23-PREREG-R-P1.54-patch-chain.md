- **Y3 — no promotion.** After both declarations, `claim P` and `claim Q` still

  is **identical before and after** the declarations.
- **Y5 — the plan stays unapplied.** `verifier.status` is `NOT_EVALUATED`, and no

## Rules

If `P` or `Q` turns out to be laned (admitted), Y2 and Y3 are falsified as
written and the card records that; no predicate is swapped to make the card pass.
Capacity first: the two candidates must be distinct triples, both endpoints
observed, and the snapshot's relation count for `file:src/a.txt` must be known
before the run.

## Limits

- One ontology version (`physis` 1.9.1) on one host; an unlaned predicate is the
  *easy* refusal, and a laned-but-wrong proposal is not tested here.
- The chain writes to a throwaway world, not to a project store.
- Coordination is one-shot: no retry policy, no authorisation step, no human
  gate. Those are product decisions this card does not make.
