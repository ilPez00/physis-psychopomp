# Plan — assumption tests and the instrument fix that A3 actually needs (2026-11-21, round 9)

Status: **PLAN**, written before execution. Predecessor: round 8.

## Why

Round 8 concluded that at ratio <= 0.4 the exact sensor likelihood cannot
distinguish any world, so the 0.67 was an artifact and GPU arms are unjustified.
That conclusion rests on **my model of the sensor**, and on the off-by-one
convention. Both are assumptions, not measurements. Also, "the honest open path
is a better instrument" is a recommendation with no number behind it.

## W1 — verify the assumptions the conclusion rests on

1. **Noise model**: the sim perturbs `pos` and `v` with uniform +-noise/2,
   independently. Measure the empirical distribution over many events: support
   is within the half-width, mean ~0, variance ~ `noise^2/12`, and the oracle log
   is untouched.
2. **The +1 convention**: for every match of the true world in a **noise-free**
   corpus, the straight-line residual must be 0 to float precision. If the
   convention is wrong anywhere, that is a test failure, not a paragraph.

Gate: both as tests; they must be able to fail (a deliberately wrong width or a
`steps` off-by-one breaks them).

## W2 — the instrument fix, measured

A3 needs the likelihood to stay discriminative **at higher noise**. Two ways to
add information without changing the combinatorics (more crossings would make
enumeration `n!` and is out of scope): observe **more samples per crossing**.
The ball is visible for several steps before it enters and after it exits, and
the log keeps none of that.

Implement `track`: the last `k=3` visible positions before the entry and the
first `k` after the exit, as an observable attribute. Then estimate velocity by
least squares over the track (noise averaged over 3 samples) and re-measure the
exact-Bayes decided rate and accuracy at ratio 0.4 and at ratio 1.

Falsifiable prediction, fixed now: if more samples per crossing reduce the
effective sensor error enough, the exact model decides some episodes at ratio 0.4
where it decided none. If it still decides none, the limiting factor is the
geometry (2-unit separation against a 5-unit sensor), not the sample count, and
the instrument needs a *much* longer window or a closer look — say so.

## W3 — record what the numbers mean for A3

Either "track(3) restores decisions at ratio 0.4: the instrument fix exists and is
cheap", or "it does not: the gap-to-noise ratio is the binding constraint", with
the measured table. Not a registered claim.

## Gate

`scripts/map-oracle.py` gains a `track` column; tests pin the decided-rate at the

## Execution status — 2026-11-21 (appended after measurement)

### W1 — the assumptions hold (with one correction)

- Noise is uniform within `+-noise/2` (max/min inside the half-width), zero mean,
  variance within the `noise^2/12` band, and the oracle attributes are identical
  with and without noise.
- The `+1` convention holds across corpora: every true match has residual `<1e-5`
  at zero noise (not exactly 0 — attributes are logged to 6 decimals, so the
  assertion is "rounding scale, not signal scale"; the 0.3-unit bias it removes
  is 30,000x larger).

### W2 — the instrument fix does not work, for a reason worth keeping

`track` (3 observable positions before the entry, 3 after the exit) is logged and
jittered, and velocity is re-estimated by least squares over it. Result:

| cell | exact Bayes (logged v) | exact Bayes (+ track) |
|---|---|---|
| 6/2 | 1.00 (decided 4, tie 2) | **empty 6** |
| 2/2 | 1.00 (decided 2, tie 4) | **empty 6** |
| 2/5 | abstains (tie 6) | **empty 6** |

Averaging position samples makes the feasibility set *empty* everywhere, because
the logged velocity is derived as `dpos/dt`: a position error of `noise/2`
becomes `noise/(2*dt)` on velocity — 10x at `dt = 0.1`. More samples of the same
noisy channel cannot fix a channel whose noise is amplified by `1/dt`.

Diagnostic (oracle velocity substituted, not shipped): still **0/6 decided at
ratio 0.4**. So velocity precision is *not* the binding constraint either —
**position noise against the 2-unit gap is** (`+-2.5` per crossing point).

A second, smaller finding: adding `track` initially re-rolled every later noise
draw and moved pinned numbers. The track now draws from its own stream, so the
log schema can grow without rewriting a pinned measurement.

### W3 — the sentence

"More samples per crossing do not restore decisions at ratio 0.4; the binding
constraint is the ratio of position noise to the gap, so the instrument fix must
change the sensor resolution, the separation, or the observable — not the sample
count." A3 stays unjustified: at ratio 0.4 the observables do not determine the
answer, and above ratio 1 a trivial statistic already wins.
