# Plan — R-P1.5 interventions and evidence labels (2026-11-21)

Status: **PLAN**, written before the run. Roadmap `docs/ROADMAP.md` R-P1.5.
Mission P1 §5: paired episodes with and without a visible velocity kick; the
four labels; no simulated event may become a historical observation.

## Predictions (fixed now)

- **P1** — a counterfactual pair is bit-identical before the kick step and
  diverges after it, for a kick on a visible ball (deterministic sim).
- **P2** — on an observable log, the oracle truth world is in INFERRED and a
  velocity-continuity-violating world is REFUTED; no false refutation of the
  truth.
- **P3** — a kick on a hidden ball is skipped by the simulator, so the
  counterfactual is identical to the observed run: there is no hidden action
  channel to intervene through.
- **P4** — `ObservationLedger` refuses any event whose provenance is not an
  executed simulation, so promotion is impossible by construction, not by
  discipline.

## Instrument

`psychopomp/interventions.py`: `Intervention(t, ball, dv)`, `paired()` (same
init, counterfactual adds the kick and carries provenance
`counterfactual:v0.1`), `classify()` (OBSERVED / INFERRED / COUNTERFACTUAL /
REFUTED), `ObservationLedger` (accepts `sim:*` only). `sim.run` gained a
`source` keyword so the counterfactual is markable at the boundary.

## Gate

`tests/test_interventions.py` (5 tests): pre-kick identity and post-kick
divergence; provenance split and ledger refusal; hidden-kick no-op; truth in
INFERRED with a refuted wrong world; ledger holds only observable executed
events. Not a registered claim.

## Execution status — 95 tests pass (appended)

All four predictions upheld:

- P1: pair identical for the first 20 steps, diverges after `t=20` kick.
- P2: truth in INFERRED; the reversed pairing (no swaps) is REFUTED.
- P3: hidden kick at `t=10` is recorded as `skipped_kicks` and the
  counterfactual observable log equals the observed one — no hidden action.
- P4: ledger raises on a counterfactual log; only `sim:*` events recorded.

Consequence for A4: interventions are usable as paired counterfactuals, and
the refusal is executable, not a convention — which is what "no simulated
event may silently become a historical observation" requires.
