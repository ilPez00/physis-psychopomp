# Preregistration — R-P1.57: matched marginals, different counterfactuals

## Fixture and evidence boundary

Compare `Y(a)=3a+S` (additive) and `Y(a)=3a+(-1)^a S` (sign-flip).
Exhaust all `a∈{0,1}` and equiprobable `S∈{-2,-1,1,2}` per law: eight cells
per law. For each factual action `a`, the alternative is `1-a` at the **same**
`S`. Give predictors only `(a,Y(a))`. Hidden `S`, true law and paired
alternative stay with the fixture/scorer, including the scoring-only oracle.
No learned arm, sampling, simulator claim, or counterfactual export.

## Predictions and controls

- **D1, capacity:** both laws have identical factual multisets at each action,
  and the same eight `(a,Y(a))` inputs give different alternatives between the
  two laws, 8/8. If the fixture misses either check, stop and report capacity
  failure without scoring.
- **D2, paired error:** predict `3(1-a)` as the factual-mean baseline; predict
  `3(1-a)+[Y(a)-3a]` for residual reuse. Their MAEs are respectively `3/2` and
  `0` on additive, `3/2` and `3` on sign-flip. The oracle using true law and
  hidden `S` has MAE `0` on both.
- **D3, shuffled control:** within each law and factual action, enumerate all
  nine no-fixed-point permutations of the four residuals. Use the permuted
  residual instead of the observed one. Every permutation preserves the
  residual multiset. Expected MAE over all 72 predictions per law: `7/3`
  additive, `4/3` sign-flip.
- **D4, abstention:** without an identified law, return both
  `3(1-a) ± [Y(a)-3a]` or abstain from a point prediction. The candidate set
  must have size two and cover 8/8 alternatives per law; no singleton point
  decision is licensed in any of the eight distinct factual inputs.
- **D5, marginal trap:** residual-reuse predictions preserve the alternative
  action's outcome multiset **in both laws**, including the sign-flip law
  where the paired error is `3`. Check marginal equality separately from MAE.

## Stop rule and scope

Exact rational comparisons, no tolerance. Report per-law denominators and
each prediction independently. Preserve failures; do not retune the
mechanisms, controls, or fixture. This is a finite counterexample to
identification from single-action marginals, not a claim that individual
counterfactuals are always unknowable.
