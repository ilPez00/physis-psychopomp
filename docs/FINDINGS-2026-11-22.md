# Findings — observer-relative identifiability (2026-11-22)

A one-page record of what this instrument measured, what it falsified, and where
the claim stops. Detail lives in `docs/LAW.md`, the sixteen preregistration files
and `research/loops/results/`. Nothing here is an assertion ahead of evidence.

## The law

> An intervention is identifiable from the observable log **iff** its event
> location at the event step lies outside the blind region `o ∩ o2(t)` — a
> **union** of rectangles when an observer has several blockers — with the
> observer held fixed across counterfactuals.

Measured: 30/30 out of sample on a boundary-free grid (13/30 unidentifiable,
exactly as predicted); 11/11 with a moving observer and both tie→unique flips;
30/30 covariant under five exact scene symmetries; 17/17 on non-convex blind sets
including a gap point identifiable *inside* the blind set's bounding box; 0
mismatches on 157 and 237 random stratified geometries. Blind **area** is only a
proxy — a differing blind spot is not sufficient (2/5 where 5/5 was required).

## The envelope

- noise: **100/100** identification to σ = 6, 94 % at σ = 10, breakpoint
  **σ\\* = 15**, 61 % at σ = 40; covered events sit at chance throughout.
- the distances **add**, they do not compete — a counterfactual corrupts both
  hypotheses alike, so the structural difference rides a shared noise floor and
  the collapse is gradual, not a cliff.
- physics: gravity (closed-form position off by 39.8 units), one collision, three
  collisions with one hidden, and a collision hidden *inside* the blind region —
  all obey the law. Same event, same step, two observer geometries: tie or unique
  **by geometry alone**.
- boundary: an event within 1e-6 of a blind edge is **excluded, never predicted**;
  accumulated position and analytic position differ at the 1e-14 level and land on
  opposite sides of the predicate.

## The observer

- a wrong self-model costs up to **3 of 4** task events (4/4 → 1/4);
- the cost tracks the **ranking flip**, not the error magnitude (`|δ| = 10` cost 3,
  `|δ| = 20` cost 2);
- it is a **cliff at certainty**: mean cost 0.60 / 0.60 / 1.80 across prior mass
  0.8 / 0.95 / 1.0 — a 5 % residual weight rescues what 20 % rescues, because the
  loss is a discrete flip and a flip needs a pure prior;
- a minimax hedge scores **3/4** where the naive worst case scores 1/4, and picks
  the **opposite** drift — but it is dominated by mild confidence (mean 0.60) and
  pays only against certain-and-wrong;
- the self-model is **learnable from the agent's own log**: estimate within one
  step, always a **subset** of the truth, `None` for an edge never crossed — so
  its errors are **one-signed** (it can believe it is less blind than it is, never
  more). The same holds for **structural** error: given the wrong number of blind
  cells, it *drops* a cell rather than merging them (R-P1.31, cost 1 of 4 events).
  Every self-model error measured here is **optimistic**. The **count** of cells
  is recoverable from the same evidence, so the structural prior is not needed —
  but a corpus that never crossed a cell yields a confidently incomplete model
  (cost 1 of 4 task events), so self-knowledge requires **sweeping the whole field
  of view**;
- **calibration replaces conservatism**: the self-measuring agent scores **4/4**,
  matching an oracle, where the fixed-wrong-belief agent scores 1/4, and the hedge
  collapses onto the calibrated pick.

## Falsified, and kept

Twelve predictions were falsified. Four of them redirected the work that produced
the supported claims:

| prediction | why it fell | what replaced it |
|---|---|---|
| "a differing blind spot is sufficient" (R-P1.10) | 2/5 on `overlap-450` | per-event coverage (R-P1.11–R-P1.12) |
| "no breakpoint to σ = 6" (R-P1.15) | there is none; the knee model was wrong | additive-margin model (R-P1.16) |
| "the grid tests hidden contacts" (R-P1.19) | 0 hidden collisions, 0 second contacts | per-cell capacity checks (R-P1.20) |
| "hedging pays" (R-P1.24, read alone) | dominated by mild confidence (R-P1.27) | scoped to certain-and-wrong (R-P1.28) |

The rest: a 1e-14 boundary cell (R-P1.11), a zero-crossing mispredicted by 2×
(R-P1.16), a self-inconsistent P4 (R-P1.23), a mis-specified defect direction
(R-P1.13), one-sided sampling (found by the conformance gate itself), the
discrete-loss shape (R-P1.21), a rounded tie (R-P1.26), and costs at full
confidence (R-P1.28).

## Method contributions

- **per-cell capacity checks** — a cell must prove it contains the event class it
  claims to test before its result counts; six cells were reported
  `DESIGN-FAILED` rather than counted;
- **standings boundary rule** — edge cells excluded by a rule declared before the
  run, not judged after;
- **generated plan tables** (`scripts/plan-table.py`) — three consecutive wrong
  predictions came from hand-reading a table, and are now impossible;
- **prereg checklist** — a prereg must name its store claim id;
- **research gates** (`scripts/research-gates.py`) — sixteen documented outcomes
  pinned, including the honest negatives, failing only on drift;
- **conformance gate** (`scripts/law-conformance.py`) — the law against
  attribution on random stratified geometries, which caught three defects in
  itself on first run.

## Where the claim stops

Rotated or curved occluders (they need polygon membership); more than two
observers; observers whose motion responds to the world; degraded object identity;
noisy self-model estimation; multi-cell self-models (the estimator returns a
single rectangle — the next card); integration (the Physis consumer side is absent
by measurement, not by assumption); and every learned arm, blocked because
deterministic identification is already at 100/100 where the law says it should be.
