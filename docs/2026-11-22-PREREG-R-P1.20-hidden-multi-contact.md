# PREREG — R-P1.20 hidden and multiple contacts, with capacity checks (2026-11-22)

Status: **registered before the run**; claim `ddf6b815` already exists in the
store (registered 2026-11-22, still a candidate). Roadmap `docs/ROADMAP.md`
R-P1.20.

R-P1.19 measured the rule under gravity and one collision (12/12 held) but its
hidden-contact and multi-contact clauses were **not tested**: the grid produced
neither. This card is built to produce them, and — the lesson from that failure —
**each cell verifies its own capacity before its result is counted**. A cell that
does not contain the event class it claims to test is reported as
`DESIGN-FAILED`, never as a verdict on the claim.

## Cells

Blind region (static observer): `o ∩ o2 = x ∈ [40.5, 60], y ∈ [35, 65]`.

**Scenario H — hidden contact.** Two bodies, lane `y = 45`: ball 0 at `x = 30`
with `v = (+3, 0)`, ball 1 at `x = 70` with `v = (−3, 0)`. They meet at `x = 50`
— inside the blind region, so the collision is **unobservable** (`ball_collide`
with `observable = False`). Capacity check: ≥ 1 hidden collision.

**Scenario M — multiple contacts.** Three bodies, lane `y = 45`: `x = (10, 38, 75)`,
`v = (+3, −3, −3)`. ball 0 and ball 1 meet at `x ≈ 24` (observable), then ball 1
travels right and meets ball 2 at `x ≈ 49.5` (hidden). Capacity check: ≥ 2
collisions **and** ≥ 1 hidden.

Each scenario × gravity `(0, 0)` or `(0, −0.5)` × event `t ∈ {30, 90}` = **8 cells**.
The kick target and coverage are read from the world's own kick-free trajectory
(the R-P1.19 rule), so the prediction does not depend on the attribution outcome.

## Predictions (fixed now)

- **P1.** In every cell whose capacity check passes, the measured tie/unique
  matches the geometric prediction. *One mismatch in a capacity-passing cell
  falsifies the rule for this physics.*
- **P2.** Capacity checks pass in **all 8** cells — the design does what it
  claims. *Falsified by any DESIGN-FAILED cell* (and that is a design receipt,
  not a claim verdict).
- **P3.** At least **4** cells contain a hidden collision, and every such cell
  obeys P1.
- **P4.** At least **2** cells contain ≥ 2 collisions (scenario M), and every
  such cell obeys P1.
- **P5 (control against triviality).** In every cell the covered case is a tie
  and the uncovered case is unique — i.e. the card does not pass by having only
  one kind of outcome. If a scenario produces only one coverage class, that is
  reported and P5 is falsified for it.

## Prior

**0.6.** R-P1.19 held 12/12 with one collision, which supports the rule; the
open risk is precisely the one this card targets — a perturbation absorbed and
re-emitted through a *hidden* collision, where two hypotheses could produce
identical observables for reasons other than coverage.

## AMENDMENT 1 — 2026-11-22, after the capacity checks fired, before any verdict

The per-cell capacity checks did exactly their job and caught a design flaw the
prereg could not see on paper: **the kick destroyed the contact structure it was
supposed to be measured against**. A kick with `dv = (0, 1.5)` on a member of the
colliding pair moves it off the partner's lane, so the collision never happens
and the cell silently tests nothing (`H, no-g, t=30`: 0 collisions).

Fix: **decouple the kicked body from the pair.** The colliding bodies stay on
lane `y = 45`; the kicked body is a separate body on lane `y = 51`, six units
away (collision radius 1, so no cross-lane contact). The contact structure is
then identical in every hypothesis, which is exactly what a clean test needs.

Corrected cells:

- **H (hidden contact), 3 bodies:** pair `x = (30, 70)`, `v = (±3, 0)` at
  `y = 45` — they meet at `x = 50` inside the blind region; kicked body
  `x = 20, v = (+3, 0)` at `y = 51`.
- **M (multiple contacts), 4 bodies:** `x = (10, 38, 75)` at `y = 45` (two
  contacts, one hidden) plus the same kicked body at `y = 51`.

Event times stay `{30, 90}`; the kicked body reaches `x = 29` (uncovered) at
`t = 30` and `x = 47` (covered) at `t = 90`, so both coverage classes are present
without touching the pair. Hypotheses, thresholds and cell count are unchanged.

### Not tested by this card, stated exactly

The conjunction **acceleration ∧ hidden contact** is untested. A card that wants
it must place the blind region where the contact actually occurs under gravity
(at `y ≈ 34` for this geometry), which is a design step that can only be done
with the trajectory in hand — and doing it *after* seeing this failure would be
retuning, so it belongs in a fresh prereg, not an amendment here.

### What did hold

A perturbation whose consequences pass through contacts that are themselves
invisible to every observer: the coverage rule still predicts identifiability
exactly, in both classes, at both event times. That was the specific way rich
physics looked most likely to break a geometric rule.
