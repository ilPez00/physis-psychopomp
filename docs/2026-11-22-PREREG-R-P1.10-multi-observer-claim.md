# PREREG — R-P1.10 multi-observer mechanism claim (2026-11-22)

Status: **registered before the run**. Roadmap `docs/ROADMAP.md` R-P1.10.
Instrument card R-P1.9 (`2026-11-22-PLAN-R-P1.9-multi-observer.md`) measured the
effect once, on one hand-picked geometry, with no seed sweep and no control.
This file fixes what would count as *generalisation* before any measurement.

## Claim under test

> An identifiability limit that comes from a blind spot is a property of the
> **observer set**, not of the world: adding a second observer whose blind spot
> differs from the first makes an otherwise unidentifiable intervention
> identifiable, and the effect is caused by blind-region reduction, not by
> observer count.

## Prior

**0.65**, and this number is honest about its own origin: R-P1.9 already
measured the effect once (single → null-tie; overlap/disjoint → unique with
margin 22.00/10.00). What this prereg adds is **generalisation** — 5 seeds × 5
observer sets, a dose-response ordering and a permuted-score control — none of
which existed in R-P1.9. A prior of 0.65, not 0.95, reflects that.

Registered in the physis store before the run; the store's embedder is
random-projection (lexical), so the registration is a record for later
refutation, not a calibrated judgement.

## Instrument

`psychopomp/sim.py` (`SimConfig.o2`, `observable`) + `psychopomp/attribution.py`.
No new module, no schema change.

Episode: parallel lanes, `Init(x=(x0, x0+4), y=(45, 51), v=((3,0),(3,0)))`,
`steps=200`. True intervention: kick `t=60, ball=0, dv=(0,1.5)` — inside the
first occluder for every seed (`x = x0+18 ∈ {42,46,50,54,58} ⊂ [40,60]`).

Seeds (the only thing that varies within a geometry): `x0 ∈ {24, 28, 32, 36, 40}`.

Candidate set per episode, declared and finite: `[true, none, visible-decoy]`
where the decoy is the same kick at `t=30` (body visible in every geometry).

Observer sets, ordered by blind area (blind = `o ∩ o2`):

| label | `o2` | blind area |
|---|---|---|
| `single` | `None` | 600 |
| `same` | `(40,60,35,65)` | 600 |
| `overlap-450` | `(45,65,35,65)` | 450 |
| `overlap-300` | `(50,70,35,65)` | 300 |
| `disjoint` | `(0,20,0,20)` | 0 |

25 cells total (5 seeds × 5 observer sets). CPU-only, seconds, no GPU, no
network.

## Hypotheses and falsifiers (fixed now)

- **H1 (main).** In every strict-coverage geometry (`overlap-450`,
  `overlap-300`, `disjoint`) the truth is unique-correct in **5/5** seeds; in
  `single` and `same` it is unique-correct in **≤ 1/5**.
  *Falsified if* any strict-coverage cell scores < 5/5, or `single`/`same`
  scores ≥ 2/5.
- **H2 (mechanism control).** Observer **count** alone does nothing: for every
  seed, `same` and `single` agree exactly on `unique`, ambiguity size,
  `d(true)` and `d(null)`.
  *Falsified if* any seed disagrees on any of those four.
- **H3 (dose–response).** Unique-correct rate is non-decreasing along
  `single, same, overlap-450, overlap-300, disjoint` with at least one strict
  increase, and the hidden step fraction strictly decreases from `single` to
  `disjoint` (with `same` equal to `single`).
  *Falsified if* the rate is non-monotone, never strictly increases, or the
  hidden fraction does not strictly fall.
- **H4 (control).** The metric is not trivially solvable: a permuted-score
  control (score values shuffled across the 3 candidates, 200 draws per cell)
  recovers the truth at **1/3 ± 0.15 in every cell**.
  *Falsified if* any cell's control recovery leaves that band.

## Ceiling rule

A cell where the single-observer arm is already unique-correct is a **ceiling**
for that seed: it cannot support H1 and is excluded from the H1 count and
reported separately. (In this construction the single arm should never be
unique on the hidden kick — if it is, the seed was not actually hidden and the
episode is mis-built, which is itself a finding to report.)

## Rules

- No retune. A falsified hypothesis is a receipt; the next card may change the
  instrument, never the threshold.
- The shuffled/permuted control is first-class: a positive that does not beat it
  is not a positive.
- Both runs (any run before this file, and this one) are reported. R-P1.9's run 1
  is already on record as an instrument failure, not a result.
- No learned arm, no GPU arm: the deterministic arms leave no headroom question
  open here, and a learned model could only match a 5/5 deterministic arm.

## Predictions registered in the store

1. H1: 5/5 unique-correct in the three strict-coverage geometries, ≤ 1/5 in
   `single` and `same`.
2. H2: `same` ≡ `single` on all 5 seeds, all four metrics.
3. H3: monotone non-decreasing accuracy with blind-area reduction, hidden step
   fraction strictly falling to 0.000.
4. H4: permuted-score control within 1/3 ± 0.15 in every cell.

## AMENDMENT 1 — 2026-11-22, before any verdict

Two construction defects in this file, found by running the grid. **No threshold,
hypothesis, arm or metric is changed**; the sections above stay as written.

1. **Seed set was invalid.** With `Init(x=(x0, x0+4))`, seeds `x0 ∈ {36, 40}`
   put ball 1 at `x = 40..44`, i.e. *inside* the first occluder at t=0, so
   `run()` refused to build the episode (`init must be observable`). The seeds
   are therefore `x0 ∈ {24, 26, 28, 30, 32}` (ball 0 at t=60 is
   `x = 42..50`, strictly inside `o`; ball 1 starts at `28..36`, strictly
   outside). The *construction* is fixed; the five-seed design is unchanged.
2. **The permuted-score control needed a tie rule.** Three of the cells have
   all candidate distances equal (that is the null-tie being measured), and the
   first implementation broke those ties lexicographically, which reported
   control recovery 0.000 in one cell. The control now draws uniformly among
   the tied minima — the only tie rule that does not smuggle a preference into
   a control.

Three cells (`single`, `x0 = 24, 28, 32`) were computed before the fix and
agreed with the null-tie prediction; they are recomputed under the amended
seeds and nothing from them is used in the verdict.

### Verdicts

- **H1 — FALSIFIED.** The prereg required 5/5 in *every* strict-coverage
  geometry. `overlap-450` scored **2/5**. The two conditions on the other side
  held (`single` 0/5, `same` 0/5).
- **H2 — SUPPORTED.** `same` is identical to `single` on all 5 seeds, all four
  metrics (unique, ambiguity, `d(true)`, `d(null)`): observer count alone does
  nothing.
- **H3 — SUPPORTED as written** (0 → 0 → 2 → 5 → 5, monotone with strict
  increases; hidden fraction 0.331 = 0.331 > 0.249 > 0.167 > 0.000), **but it is
  the wrong variable** — see below.
- **H4 — SUPPORTED.** Control 0.295–0.410 against 1/3 ± 0.15 in every cell.

### Why H1 failed, and what the data actually says

Not a threshold artifact and not a weak effect — a **wrong predictor**. The
`overlap-450` failures are exactly the seeds whose kick location stays blind:
the residual blind region is `x ∈ [45, 60]`, the kick at `t=60` happens at
`x = x0 + 18 ∈ {42, 44, 46, 48, 50}`, so seeds with `x ≥ 46` (3 of 5) are
hidden and tie with the null, while `x ∈ {42, 44}` are visible and unique —
**2/5**, exactly as measured. `overlap-300` (blind `x ∈ [50,60]`) leaves only
`x = 50` on the boundary and resolves 5/5.

So the mechanism is **per-event coverage**, not aggregate blind area:

> An intervention is identifiable iff its *location* is covered by at least one
> observer; blind area is only a proxy for that, and a strictly smaller blind
> region that still covers the event changes nothing.

H3's monotone rate is a consequence of this rule on this geometry sweep, not
independent evidence for "more coverage is better". That distinction is why H1
is recorded as falsified rather than quietly re-scoped to H3.

### Consequences

- Registered hypothesis `2661e5ab-01a3-426f-a19b-3c5cae433f06` moves to
  **Contradicted** as stated ("adding a second observer whose blind spot
  differs … makes it identifiable"): differing blind spots are necessary but
  **not sufficient**; the event location must be uncovered.
- The reformulated claim (per-event coverage) is **not** claimed on this data:
  it was derived *after* seeing the failures, so it needs its own prereg on a
  fresh geometry grid (R-P1.11).
- No retune of H1's threshold. The 5/5-per-geometry rule was the point: it is
  what caught the over-general claim.
