Status: **registered before the run**. Roadmap `docs/ROADMAP.md` R-P1.16.
R-P1.15 found no breakpoint up to σ = 6 and rewrote the model: `d(true)` and
`d(null)` **add**, they do not compete. What survives is the additive margin
`d(null) − d(true)` = 24.8, 24.1, 23.8, 22.8, 21.5, 20.8, 20.2 for
σ = 0.75 … 6, falling about **0.8 per unit σ**. This card tests that line where

## Prediction, derived from the R-P1.15 margin line (before the run)

Linear margin `M(σ) ≈ 25.4 − 0.85σ` reaches zero at **σ ≈ 30**. Accuracy should
therefore stay high well past σ = 6, begin to slip when the margin's *spread*
becomes comparable to the margin itself, and collapse near σ ≈ 30.

Grid: **σ ∈ {10, 15, 20, 30, 40}**, same geometry, cells, metric, and 100
realizations per σ with `episode = 1000·σ_index + r`. 1000 episodes.

Note the physical absurdity deliberately: σ = 10 means ±5 units of sensor noise
per coordinate, on a 2-unit-radius body in 1-unit lanes. The point is to bracket
the collapse, not to describe a plausible camera.

## Predictions (fixed now)

- **P1 (margin is linear).** A least-squares fit of `d(null) − d(true)` over the
  five new points has slope in **[-1.2, -0.4]** and intercept implying zero
  margin between σ = 20 and σ = 45.
  *Falsified if* the slope leaves that band or the zero-crossing leaves [15, 60].
- **P2 (accuracy survives to σ = 10).** Rank accuracy at σ = 10 is **≥ 0.90**.
  *Falsified otherwise.*
- **P3 (collapses by σ = 40).** Rank accuracy at σ = 40 is **< 0.70**, and
  accuracy is non-increasing across the five levels.
  *Falsified if* σ = 40 stays ≥ 0.70 or the sequence rises.
- **P4 (the breakpoint is bracketed).** The first σ with accuracy < 0.90 lies in
  **[15, 40]**, consistent with the σ ≈ 30 extrapolation.
- **P5 (coverage still untouched).** The covered cell stays within 0.5 ± 0.15 at
  every σ in the grid.
  *Falsified by* any covered cell outside the band.*

## Prior

**0.5.** The margin line is measured and nearly straight over seven points, which
supports P1/P2; but the collapse is a tail behaviour of a summed distance, and
extrapolating a straight line through a variance-dominated regime is exactly the
kind of extrapolation that should be assumed wrong until measured.

## Rules

  extrapolated and that is the answer.
- The grid is fixed here; no σ added afterwards.

### The claim, final form

> Identifiability of an intervention is **per-event observer coverage**, and it
> is robust to sensor noise out to the point where the noise floor exceeds the
